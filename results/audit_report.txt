Recommendations
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Do not deploy as-is: 22 subgroups fail the four-fifths rule, worst being Female_Asian-Pac-Islander_Senior (>50), Female_Amer-Indian-Eskimo_Young (<30), Male_Amer-Indian-Eskimo_Young (<30).
  2. Apply group-aware post-processing (per-subgroup thresholds or equalised odds) and re-audit at the intersectional level, not just per attribute.
  3. Equal-opportunity gap of -0.2576 for Male_White_Young (<30) (n_positives=149): qualified members of this subgroup are being missed, which threshold tuning alone will not fix.
  4. 3 subgroups are too small to audit (n < 10). Oversample them before claiming the model is fair for everyone.
  5. Report intersectional metrics alongside the single-attribute ones; the marginal view averages away exactly the harms this audit surfaces.
